## California governor wants an AI kill switch for frontier models

Gavin Newsom issued an executive order directing California to convene experts who'll recommend within two months how to mandate emergency shutoffs for advanced AI systems. The state is positioning itself to lead AI oversight after Newsom vetoed a similar bill last year.

*California could set the template for how states regulate AI model deployment and safety controls.*
