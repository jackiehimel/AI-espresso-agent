## Google's AI now translates sign language straight to text on your phone

DeepMind released SL2T, a model that converts American Sign Language to text in real time using your phone's camera. It's rolling out in beta for Android and iOS, letting Deaf and hard-of-hearing users text, search, and communicate without typing. The model runs on-device, so it works offline and keeps signing private.

*First mainstream sign language AI that actually ships to consumers, not just a research demo.*

[Google DeepMind Blog](https://deepmind.google/blog/putting-sign-language-ai-into-users-hands/) · Aug 14
