## Claude will now invisibly watermark every AI-generated text and image

Anthropic is embedding machine-readable watermarks in Claude's text outputs and cryptographically signed metadata in images to comply with EU transparency rules. The markers are invisible to humans but detectable by software—meaning every Claude response and generated file will carry proof of its AI origin.

*First major model to watermark text at scale, setting a precedent other labs may follow.*

[The Verge — AI](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/977823/anthropic-claude-ai-watermarks-c2pa-text-images) · Aug 12

## NVIDIA's new open model runs AI agents cheaper and longer

Nemotron 3.5 Lightning is built specifically for long-running agents that need to stay active without burning through compute. *Agentic workflows get expensive fast; this makes them viable at scale.*
