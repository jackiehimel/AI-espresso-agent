## Anthropic mathematician just solved a 167-year-old math problem

Levent Alpöge, who works at Anthropic, claims he's proved the Riemann hypothesis—one of math's biggest unsolved problems about how prime numbers are distributed. He wrote the proof in Lean, a formal verification language, so computers can check it's valid. Other mathematicians have confirmed the code compiles, though it'll take time to verify the logic holds.

*If it stands, it's one of the biggest math breakthroughs in a century—and it happened at an AI lab.*

## OpenAI blocked 99.6% of attacks trying to steal GPT's hidden reasoning

OpenAI's new defenses stop attackers from extracting the chain-of-thought steps their models use to reach answers. In red-team tests, success rates for stealing this protected reasoning dropped from 54% to 0.2%. The company is treating distillation attacks—where bad actors use model outputs to train unfiltered clones—like biosecurity threats.

*If attackers can steal reasoning traces, they can build uncensored versions of frontier models.*

[OpenAI News](https://openai.com/index/combatting-adversarial-distillation) · Nov 21

## Signal just blocked Siri and Gemini from reading your chats

Signal disabled screenshots on iOS and Android by default to stop AI agents from scanning messages. Users can turn screenshots back on in settings, but Signal's president says OS makers aren't giving developers good tools to block agent access without breaking core features.

*Apps now have to choose between user privacy and basic functions like screenshots.*

[The Verge — AI](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1038390/signal-blocking-ai-agent-screenshots-mobile-app) · Nov 21
