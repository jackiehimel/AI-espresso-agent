## ChatGPT now falls under EU's strictest tech regulations

The EU just classified ChatGPT as a Very Large Online Search Engine under the Digital Services Act, meaning OpenAI must now actively mitigate risks around minors, user mental health, and illegal content. The rules apply the same accountability framework used for Google and Bing to AI chat.

*First major AI chatbot to get the same regulatory treatment as traditional search engines.*
