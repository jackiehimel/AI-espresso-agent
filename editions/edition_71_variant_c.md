## Cerebras just shipped a chip with 4.4 trillion transistors on one wafer

The CS-4 is a wafer-scale processor built on a single 300mm silicon wafer with 4.4 trillion transistors and 21 million cores. It's designed to train large AI models without splitting computation across multiple chips, eliminating the networking overhead that slows down traditional GPU clusters.

*Training runs could get faster and cheaper if you never have to shard your model across hundreds of GPUs.*
