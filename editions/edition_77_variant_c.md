## OpenAI is cutting off Cursor after SpaceX bought it for $60B

OpenAI will stop letting Cursor access its models on Nov. 12, weeks after Elon Musk's SpaceX acquired the AI coding tool for $60 billion. OpenAI cited concerns about the acquisition but didn't specify what those concerns are.

*The split shows how quickly AI partnerships can unravel when competitors change hands.*

Adobe's rolling out a beta view that puts all of Photoshop's AI tools—prompt-based editing, background removal, image extension—in one dedicated toolbar. It's optional, so you can flip between the classic interface and the AI-focused one depending on what you're building.

## Google Search can now book your entire trip in AI Mode

Google added three features to AI Mode in Search: you can now get full itineraries with restaurant and activity recommendations, compare flight and hotel options side-by-side with live pricing, and book directly without leaving the results page. It's rolling out in the US first, with hotels bookable through Google's travel partners.

*Search is becoming the actual booking agent, not just the research tool.*
