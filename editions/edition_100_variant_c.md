## Sony and UMG sue Suno again, claiming its new model trained on pirated outputs

Sony and Universal filed another lawsuit against AI music startup Suno, alleging its v6 model was trained on outputs from earlier models — which themselves learned from copyrighted songs ripped from YouTube. *Training new models on old model outputs might not dodge copyright claims.*
