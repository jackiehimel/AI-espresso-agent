## Microsoft's AI tool diagnoses medical cases 4x better than doctors

Microsoft's Diagnostic Orchestrator uses multiple AI agents to mimic a medical panel — ordering tests, reviewing symptoms, and reaching diagnoses. On complex cases from medical journals, it hit 83% accuracy versus 36% for doctors with references and 22% without. The company published its benchmark, SDBench, in Science and plans to open-source it.

*AI might finally deliver on years of healthcare promises, moving from research to tools doctors could actually use.*

[The Verge — AI](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1016690/suleyman-microsoft-diagnostic-orchestrator-science-medical-superintelligence) · Oct 23

## Anthropic donates Agent Skills standard to Linux Foundation

Agent Skills, the open format that teaches AI agents specialized tasks through simple markdown files, is moving to the Agentic AI Foundation under Linux. Since launching in December, 70+ platforms including ChatGPT, Cursor, and GitHub Copilot have integrated it, with 1 million skills published and 280 million installed.

*A vendor-neutral home means agents can share specialized knowledge across platforms without lock-in.*

[Anthropic News](https://www.anthropic.com/news/donating-agent-skills-to-the-agentic-ai-foundation) · Oct 23

## 1Password lets AI agents log into your accounts without ever seeing your password

When an agent tries to log in on your behalf, 1Password sends you an alert to approve it, then fills in the credentials itself—the agent never touches your actual password. Works with Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, and others. Launching the same day U.S. banks started detecting agents inside customer accounts.

*Agents need access to do useful work, but handing over passwords creates permanent risk—this splits the difference.*

[TechCrunch — AI](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/1password-launches-a-way-for-ai-agents-to-log-in-on-your-behalf-without-seeing-your-password/) · Oct 23
