## ChatGPT now watermarks its text output — but only in Europe

OpenAI is rolling out invisible, machine-readable watermarks in ChatGPT and Codex responses for EU users. The textGrain system works like Google's SynthID and Anthropic's approach, embedding patterns that identify AI-generated text without changing how it looks or reads.

*Watermarking makes AI text detectable without breaking the user experience or limiting what models can say.*
